Why Study in Italy?

Italy combines world-class academic prestige, centuries-old university traditions, state-subsidized affordable tuition, and high post-study European career mobility.

🏛

Centuries of Academic Prestige

Home to the oldest universities in the Western world (Bologna est. 1088, Padua est. 1222). Italian universities consistently rank among the top worldwide for Engineering, Architecture, Medicine, and Social Sciences.

💶

World's Most Generous Scholarships

Through regional financial aid bodies (DSU, ER.GO, Disco Lazio, EDiSU), eligible international students can study with 100% tuition exemption, free campus dining meals, and cash stipends up to €8,500/year.

🌍

500+ Programs Taught in English

Study Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD programs entirely in the English language without requiring prior Italian knowledge. Programs span AI, Data Science, Mechanical Engineering, Business, and Medicine (IMAT).

🛂

Schengen Visa & European Mobility

An Italian student residence permit (Permesso di Soggiorno) grants visa-free travel and exploration across all 29 European Schengen countries during your academic journey.

💼

Legal Part-Time Work Rights

International students are legally permitted to work up to 20 hours per week during semesters (1,040 hours per year) and can convert their permit to a 1-year job search permit upon graduation.

📜

Globally Accredited Bologna Process

Italian university degrees adhere to the European Higher Education Area (Bologna Process), ensuring instant worldwide equivalency and recognition for further study or professional licensing.

Italian Scholarships & Financial Aid

Italy offers some of the most comprehensive student financial assistance programs in Europe. Learn how regional and merit grants can make your education virtually free.

Most Popular • Constitutional Right

Regional Scholarships (DSU / ER.GO / Disco Lazio)

Coverage: 100% Tuition Fee Exemption + Up to €8,500/year Cash Grant + Free Canteen Meals + Free Student Accommodation.
  • Eligibility: Based on family economic situation (ISEE Parificato threshold under €25,000–€27,000/year).
  • Who Can Apply: Admitted international Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD students from non-EU countries.
  • Required Documents: Family composition certificate, annual gross family income, property ownership/rent documents, and bank balance on December 31st.
  • Document Legalization: All documents must be translated into Italian and legalized or Apostilled.
Deadline: June – August Annually Get ISEE Guidance on WhatsApp
Government Merit Award

MAECI Scholarships (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)

Coverage: €900/month stipend + Full University Tuition Fee Exemption + Health Insurance Coverage.
  • Eligibility: Outstanding international students pursuing Master's or PhD degrees in Italian public institutions.
  • Age Limit: Maximum 28 years old for Master's programs, 30 years old for PhD programs.
  • Evaluation: Based strictly on academic merit, research proposal, CV, and motivation letter.
  • Duration: Granted for 6 to 9 months, renewable based on academic progress.
Deadline: April – June Annually Inquire MAECI Details ↗
Institutional Merit

University Merit Awards (PoliMi, UniBo, UniPd)

Coverage: Platinum & Gold Awards up to €10,000/year OR €11,000 cash grants (UniBo Action 2) + Tuition waivers.
  • PoliMi Merit: Automatic evaluation during Master's application rounds for high GPA applicants.
  • UniBo Action 1 & 2: Awarded to top international students taking the SAT (Bachelor's) or GRE (Master's).
  • Padua International Excellence: 50+ scholarships of €8,000/year awarded on pure academic merit.

Structured Roadmap

Step-by-Step Admission to Italy

A transparent breakdown of the exact procedural milestones from initial profile review to landing at an Italian airport.

Phase 1 • Nov – April

Profile Evaluation & University Application

We analyze your academic marks, match you with top accredited English-taught degrees, prepare your Academic CV and Motivation Letter, and submit direct university applications.

Phase 2 • Jan – May

Offer Letter & CIMEA Verification

Upon receiving your university acceptance / eligibility letter, we initiate your CIMEA Statement of Comparability and Verification or Embassy Declaration of Value (DoV).

Phase 3 • April – July

Universitaly Pre-Enrollment Portal

Registration and submission of your pre-enrollment application on the official Italian Ministry portal (Universitaly.it). The university validates your dossier and forwards it to the Italian Embassy.

Phase 4 • June – August

Regional Scholarship Application (ISEE Parificato)

Preparation and Italian sworn translation of family financial certificates (income, property, bank balance) and online submission to the respective regional scholarship body (DSU/ER.GO).

Phase 5 • June – August

Italian Embassy National Student Visa (Type D)

Compilation of your official visa file: Universitaly Summary, bank maintenance certificate (~€6,000 funds), accommodation proof, health insurance, flight reservation, and embassy interview coaching.

Phase 6 • September – October

Arrival in Italy, Residence Permit & Codice Fiscale

Pre-departure briefing, flight to Italy, applying for your Italian Tax Code (Codice Fiscale), Permesso di Soggiorno kit at the Italian Post Office, opening your bank account, and university enrollment finalization.
